# Feature Readiness Plan

## Feature and scope

Summarize the feature goal, required behavior, constraints, and explicit exclusions.

## Verified current behavior

Describe the current flow relevant to the feature and cite the supporting files or symbols.

## Likely change points

For each change point, include:

- **File or symbol:** The source location.
- **Responsibility:** What it currently owns.
- **Expected involvement:** Why the feature may affect it.
- **Confidence:** Verified or unresolved.

## Dependencies and interoperability

Identify dependencies between the UI, Swift wrapper, Objective-C implementation, persistence, and attachment storage.

## Risks and safeguards

For each meaningful risk, include its evidence, potential impact, and proposed safeguard or investigation.

## Testing and validation

List existing test support, missing coverage, and the automated or manual checks needed before and during implementation.

## Open questions

Record unanswered product, design, data, and engineering questions without guessing.

## Independent review

Summarize the review findings and their supporting evidence.

## Human decisions

Record which recommendations were accepted, revised, rejected, or left unresolved and explain why.

## Readiness recommendation

State whether the feature is ready for detailed development planning, what must be resolved first, and what remains explicitly out of scope.
