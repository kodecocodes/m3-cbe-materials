# Lesson 1 Prompts: Explore the Codebase and Evaluate Technical Debt

These three prompts are short enough to type during the lesson. The file is provided as an optional copyable reference.

## Prompt 1: Explore and Assess

```text
Explore BabyTrack. Map its entry points, screens, data flow, dependencies, and tests, then identify its most important potential technical debt. Cite files and clearly label anything uncertain.
```

Inspect a few important citations and tell the AI about any corrections.

## Prompt 2: Verify and Prioritize

```text
Recheck your findings against the source. Remove unsupported claims, separate actual debt from age or style preferences, and prioritize verified issues as address now, plan later, or monitor. Explain the tradeoffs briefly.
```

Accept, reject, or revise the priorities before continuing.

## Prompt 3: Create the Assessment

```text
Create the complete Markdown for `codebase-assessment.md` from the verified findings and my corrections. Include the codebase map, prioritized debt, rejected or unverified claims, open questions, and next investigation. Cite the source, do not propose implementation work, and return only the Markdown file.
```
